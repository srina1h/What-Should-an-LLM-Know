# Education and income in human-development measurement

The UNDP Human Development Report 2019 technical notes treat health,
education, and income as distinct dimensions. Its education calculation uses
mean and expected years of schooling; its income calculation uses gross
national income per capita on a logarithmic scale. These definitions give
background for interpreting country-level development measures.

The technical notes do **not** prove that this benchmark's `Schooling+` or
`Income_composition_of_resources+` columns were copied from the 2019 UNDP
series, use the same formulas, or have the same units. No formula or hard
constraint for either CSV objective is imported here.
